⚔️ Module 03 Timed Practice: The Subjunctive Mood

Welcome to the ludus (the Roman word for both "school" and "game"). Face 10 quick questions on the present and imperfect subjunctive, sequence of tenses, this module's vocabulary, and the Plautus reading from Mīles Glōriōsus, with 90 seconds on the clock. With the clock running there is no time to look anything up, so trust your training, soldier.

⏱ How scoring works

Ten questions. Ninety seconds. Three attempts. Your best of the three attempts is the score you record. Up to 90 points come from answering correctly, so take the time you need to read carefully. Finishing with time to spare earns a speed bonus of up to 10 points. Accuracy first; speed gives you the edge. Questions you do not reach before time runs out count as missed.
