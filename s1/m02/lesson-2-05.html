Write your own: make one Latin sentence meaning “The slave follows the master and uses a trick.” Use sequor and ūtor with the correct cases. Then reveal one correct version.

Servus dominum sequitur et dolō ūtitur. — dominum is accusative (sequor takes a direct object), but dolō is ablative because ūtor governs the ablative. Both verbs are 3rd sg. deponent, translated actively.
