Your single PDF should contain, in order: (1) English Profile — biography, achievement, and reception; (2) Latin Translation — your authentic 4–8 line passage with citation and English rendering; (3) Etymology — key words traced to English descendants; (4) Process Reflection — how you researched and what you found.

✅ TRY IT · the method
What is the FIRST move when translating an authentic sentence?

Commit before revealing: in one sentence, state your figure, your passage's source, and one English derivative you'll feature. Say it, then reveal a model.

"My figure is Cicero; my passage is six lines from De Amicitiā 20; and I'll feature consensus, from cōnsēnsiō (agreement/harmony)." If you can say a sentence like that with confidence, your project has a spine — now write it out.
