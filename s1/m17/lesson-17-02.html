📌 PART 3 · THE THREE ANGLES 10 min

How to study a Roman figure

A strong figure study looks at its subject from three directions. Keep all three in view as you research.

1 · BIOGRAPHY & ACHIEVEMENT

What did this person do? What made them historically significant? What primary sources — Latin texts — exist from or about them?

2 · RECEPTION

How has this figure been interpreted — by later Romans, by Renaissance humanists, by modern historians? Are they controversial?

3 · LANGUAGE

What Latin vocabulary and texts are associated with this figure? Can you find an authentic Latin passage — from their own writing or a contemporary's — that illuminates their character or significance?

Your project requires a Latin primary-source text of about 4–8 lines to translate. It does not have to be difficult Latin — but it must be authentic: a primary source, not a later summary. Tomorrow you'll learn exactly how to find and vet one.
