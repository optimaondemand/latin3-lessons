📚 PART 1 · THE COMPLETE RULE 13 min

All four tenses in one grid

With all four subjunctive tenses now available, the sequence of tenses is complete. The main verb's tense determines the sequence; the subordinate subjunctive tense expresses when the subordinate action occurs relative to the main verb.

📌 RULE 3 · FULL SEQUENCE

Primary sequence (main verb: present / future / future perfect): subordinate action at the same time → present subjunctive; subordinate action before → perfect subjunctive.  ·  Secondary sequence (main verb: imperfect / perfect / pluperfect): subordinate action at the same time → imperfect subjunctive; subordinate action before → pluperfect subjunctive.

SequenceMain verb tenseSame timeBefore main verb
PrimaryPresent · Future · Fut. Perf.present subj.perfect subj.
SecondaryImperfect · Perfect · Pluperfectimperfect subj.pluperfect subj.
📘 EXAMPLE · SEQUENCE IN ACTION

Rogat quid faciant (primary, same time): “He asks what they are doing.”  ·  Rogat quid fēcerint (primary, before): “He asks what they did.”  ·  Rogāvit quid facerent (secondary, same time): “He asked what they were doing.”  ·  Rogāvit quid fēcissent (secondary, before): “He asked what they had done.”

Read the four examples again as a set. Notice how only the main verb and the subjunctive tense change — the clause structure is identical. That is the whole rule in miniature: pick the row from the main verb, pick the column from the time relationship.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
Which half of the sequence rule takes more thought for you right now?

Commit first: in Trīmalchiō rogāvit num servī omnia parāvissent ("Trimalchio asked whether the slaves had prepared everything"), which sequence is this, and why is parāvissent the right tense? Say your answer, then reveal.

Secondary sequence, action before. The main verb rogāvit is perfect → secondary sequence. The preparing happened before the asking → the "before" column of the secondary row = pluperfect subjunctive, so parāvissent is exactly right.
