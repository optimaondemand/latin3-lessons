✅ TRY IT · vocabulary
Which word means “natural talent, genius, mind”?

Write your own: compose one Latin relative clause of characteristic meaning “There is no one who does not praise glory.” Use a trigger phrase and a present subjunctive. Then reveal one correct version.

Nēmō est quī laudem nōn laudet. — nēmō est quī (characteristic trigger) + laudet (present subjunctive). Other verbs work too, e.g. …quī glōriam nōn cupiat. If you wrote an indicative (laudat), remember: characteristic clauses always take the subjunctive.
