Write your own: give a one-sentence rule for choosing between a cum historical and a cum concessive clause. Commit to it, then reveal one good version.

Both take the subjunctive, so look to the main clause: if it contains tamen (“nevertheless”), read cum as concessive (“although”); with no tamen and a plain past narrative, read it as historical/circumstantial (“when”). Context and logic settle the rare ambiguous case.
