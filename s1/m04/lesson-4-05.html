✅ TRY IT · sequence
After the present-tense main verb venit (“he comes”), a purpose clause uses which subjunctive?

Compose your own: write one Latin sentence meaning “He was so rich that everyone was astonished” — a result clause. Then reveal one correct version.

Tam dīves erat ut omnēs attonitī essent. — the intensity word tam in the main clause signals a result clause; ut + imperfect subjunctive (essent) follows the past main verb erat. If you wrote ita dīves erat ut… that works equally well.
